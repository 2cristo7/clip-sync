# Quick Start

Get clipboard syncing between your devices in under a minute.

## Step 1 — Install

Download and install ClipSync on each device you want to sync. See the [install guide](install.md) for your platform.

## Step 2 — Launch and onboard

Open ClipSync. A friendly wizard walks you through setup:

![Welcome screen](screenshots/welcome.png)

## Step 3 — Pair your devices

If your devices are on the same Wi-Fi network, they find each other automatically. You'll see a prompt:

> "Found 'Pablo's MacBook' on this network. Pair?"

Tap **Pair** on both devices — done!

![Discovery screen](screenshots/discovery.png)

**Not on the same network?** Use "Add by IP" and enter the other device's IP address (or Tailscale IP for remote access).

## Step 4 — Copy and paste

That's it! Copy something on one device, and it appears on all your paired devices instantly.

![Synced clipboard](screenshots/synced.png)

## Tips

- The tray icon shows your sync status at a glance
- Pause syncing anytime from the home screen or tray menu
- Drag a file onto the ClipSync window to send it to your other devices
